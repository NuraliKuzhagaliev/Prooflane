# Independent initial competition review — 3 November

The initial review distinguished a working bounded arithmetic/runtime prototype from a complete competition entry. It challenged the necessity of generated code for a fixed formula, prepared contract-price input, unmeasured business benefit and broad originality claims. Receipts and sandboxing alone were not sufficient differentiation.

Required next evidence included a declared assumption comparison with original preserved, portable semantic checking, a coherent browser demo and real Vultr/provider/public deployment. At that date external billing/access gates remained blocked. Official criterion names were used without fabricated weights or a guarantee of winning.
