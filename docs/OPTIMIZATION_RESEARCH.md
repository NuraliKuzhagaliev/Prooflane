# Historical optimization research — 3 November

The first optimization research challenged generic “CSV agent” positioning against spreadsheet what-if analysis, planning systems and data assistants. It selected a declared-assumption comparison with preserved original, exact deltas, source lineage and offline verification. It did not establish market uniqueness or customer ROI.

The evidence contract required the original mode/owner/hash, exactly one declared numeric edit, complete parsed-source comparison (including notes), unchanged baseline and a separate verified scenario. Failed scenarios could not expose a verified comparison. A rehashed packet with undeclared edits had to fail semantic reconstruction. Later case work broadened this to shared/joint alternatives; that later contract must not be backdated into this research.

Budget remained0 with existing Vultr credits; provider/VM/billing access was then blocked. Local fixed Docker and doubles could not close actual cloud/model gates. Proposals for pilot users, willingness to pay and comparative tasks remained unexecuted. The research recommended showing a reviewer decision rather than a generic summary, while keeping containment as an observable requirement.
