# Historical six-area opportunity research — 8 November

The research considered36 candidates across user value, efficiency, technical depth, clarity, differentiation and reliability, then selected18 implementable changes against existing evidence. It consulted primary AP/analysis descriptions, including Ramp, AppZen and MindBridge; matching, duplicate checks and explainability were treated as established capabilities.

Chosen work: usable CSV/brief handoff, shared-contract grouping; local OAT/residual recalculation and indexed matching; model-selected bounded guidance, observable pre-choice consequences and stricter verification; direct Reference onboarding, CSV help/recovery and saved navigation; duplicate financial-row warnings and explicit excluded monetary scope; durable operation IDs, per-case locks and strict JSON.

The research required measured equivalence before accepting performance changes. The same synthetic500/500 fixture,2 warmups and9 samples with full-response hashes were used. These are local CPU comparisons, not internet latency, model latency or a cloud benchmark. Customer value, probabilities and saved money were not measured.

The research output is a dated decision aid, not a new independent review conducted for the final video. [Implemented result](TRANSFORMATION_RESULT_20261108.md), [reliability contract](TRANSFORMATION_RELIABILITY_20261108.md).
