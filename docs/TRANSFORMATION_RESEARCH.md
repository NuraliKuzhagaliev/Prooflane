# Historical product transformation research — 3–4 November

The research identified three limitations: the contract price was embedded in the invoice CSV, one-cell what-if could not express linked assumptions, and hashes alone did not establish input truth. Adding another graph or summary would not address those limitations.

The selected scope was two dated sources, explicit overlap/exclusion decisions, shared contract variables, joint alternatives, exact conditional ranges and a reconstructible case. Alternative directions and established products were compared through first-party sources; no undocumented competitor absence or scientific novelty was inferred.

The proposed arithmetic remained positive-only price variance in cents with positive integer quantities. Exact interval endpoints are feasible corners under declared independent target ranges. One-at-a-time sensitivity deltas must not be summed into a joint result. A shared price is one variable across dependent rows; source truth and probability are outside the math.

Independent evaluation later reconstructed baseline768, shared368, joint568 and ranges148..1193 /148..1993, rejected semantic forgeries and corrected an overclaim about next-evidence utility. Local execution and packet checks were real; VSI/public release and customer studies remained separate gates at that date. [Current evidence](../TESTING.md).
