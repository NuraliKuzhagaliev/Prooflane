# Reference principles and three design concepts — historical studio record

The template loop came from polishing the inherited rail/card hierarchy rather than changing the product's organizing objects. Source decisions, scope and a reproducible review needed to determine the composition.

Seven primary references informed distinct principles: Mercury Books task-led accounting; Ramp AP's connected workflow; Linear's divergent design process; Stripe's disclosure/confirmation states; Instrument/ServiceNow's identity across type/data/motion; Pentagram/SMCC's display/text typography separation; Observable Plot's purposeful marks. Their layouts, brand colors, custom fonts, performance claims and banking/OCR/payment capabilities were not copied into Prooflane.

**Evidence Atlas**: relationship canvas with source ports and traceable edges, contextual inspection, spatial navigation. Strong provenance metaphor but graph orientation,500-row scale, mobile and keyboard equivalents create substantial risk.

**Audit Folio**: named chapters, ruled source register, adjacent annotation and anchored original. Selection continues from source to ledger/assumption/proof. Typographic/document rhythm fits actual invoice review and semantic HTML. Chosen for fit and feasibility, with explicit rejection of faux-paper textures/stamps or oversized ornamental headings.

**Comparison Theatre**: pinned original and paired quantitative tracks, synchronized comparisons and staged disclosure. Strong amount comparison, but alternatives can dominate unresolved evidence and complicate starting a review.

The authored vector sheets use comparable1500×820 entry/loaded compositions; they are sketches, not working apps. The director and independent UX reviewer scored them separately. Folio's strongest common judgments were product fit9/10 and feasibility9/10; originality potential did not override readable task flow. [UX review](UX_AND_PROCESS_AUDIT.md), [implementation/limits](../../DESIGN_REPORT.md).

Implementation guidance: local Newsreader headings, neutral controls, IBM Plex Mono references; display36–44px, body14–16px, essential labels at least12px. Actual browser rendering must verify fallback/mockup assumptions. Shared source identity matters more than ornamental animations. No fake intermediate numbers, hover-only essentials, drag-only tasks, generic hero/card stack or decorative chart.

The original warm annotation palette was later replaced at the user's request with blue-gray/teal and decorative slashes removed. The reference concepts remain a historical decision record, not current screenshots. Local fonts retain OFL licenses.
